## ICP

Small/medium waste operators in England & Wales who produce, carry, or
receive controlled waste and currently run on paper transfer notes:
skip-hire firms, small carriers/brokers/dealers, transfer stations,
construction firms with carrier registrations. 1–25 staff, no compliance
officer; the owner is the duty-holder.

## Statutory date + citation

- **1 October 2026** — digital waste tracking (DWT) becomes mandatory.
  Environment Act 2021 s.58; Waste (Miscellaneous Amendments)
  Regulations. Duty of Care: EPA 1990 s.34; Waste (England and Wales)
  Regulations 2011.
- **Two working days** — window to record a movement in the DWT service.
- Quarterly/annual permit returns to the EA for permitted sites.
