## ICP

**Primary (Oct 2026 wave): permitted RECEIVING sites** — transfer
stations, treatment facilities, skip-hire firms with their own permitted
sites. The Digital Waste Tracking (England) Regulations 2026 put the
recording duty on the operator receiving the waste (see
docs/dwt-defra-api.md). 1–25 staff, no compliance officer; the owner is
the duty-holder.

**Second wave (Oct 2027): carriers/brokers/dealers** — build the list
now, convert them when their mandate lands; carrier private beta runs
from autumn 2026.

## Statutory date + citation

- **1 October 2026** — digital waste tracking mandatory for receiving
  sites, England & Wales (The Digital Waste Tracking (England)
  Regulations 2026; Scotland & NI Jan 2027; carriers Oct 2027).
  Environment Act 2021 s.58; Waste (Miscellaneous Amendments)
  Regulations. Duty of Care: EPA 1990 s.34; Waste (England and Wales)
  Regulations 2011.
- **Two working days** — window to record a movement in the DWT service.
- Quarterly/annual permit returns to the EA for permitted sites.
