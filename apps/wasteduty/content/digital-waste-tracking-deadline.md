# Digital waste tracking deadline: the dates that matter

_Rules version: The Digital Waste Tracking (England) Regulations 2026 (draft SI as published on legislation.gov.uk) · Last reviewed: 8 August 2026_

There isn't one digital waste tracking (DWT) deadline — there are two kinds. A **calendar deadline** that decides when your business enters the regime, and a **rolling deadline** (two working days per movement) that never stops once you're in.

## The calendar deadlines

Sources: [The Digital Waste Tracking (England) Regulations 2026](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukdsi/2026/9780348282726); [gov.uk DWT service page](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/digital-waste-tracking-service).

Note where the October 2026 duty sits: on the **receiver**. If you run a transfer station, treatment facility, or a skip yard with its own environmental permit, 1 October 2026 is your date. Carriers without a permitted site get an extra year.

## The rolling deadline: two working days

From your mandate date, every load of controlled waste received must be recorded in the DWT service **by the end of the second working day following receipt** ([Digital Waste Tracking (England) Regulations 2026](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukdsi/2026/9780348282726)).

Worked examples (assuming Mon–Fri working days, no bank holidays):

- Received Monday → record by end of Wednesday
- Received Thursday → record by end of Monday
- Received Friday → record by end of Tuesday

## A readiness countdown for receiving sites

- **Now (August 2026):** confirm scope, map your weighbridge/intake data against the mandatory receipt fields, register for the service or choose software.
- **September 2026:** run a dry month — record every receipt as if live; verify carrier registrations for all regular carriers and keep the check evidence.
- **1 October 2026:** live. Every receipt is a legal record with a two-working-day clock on it.
- **October onward:** watch the backlog daily; a missed movement doesn't announce itself.

## FAQ

**Has the DWT deadline moved before?**
Yes — earlier gov.uk updates referenced an April 2026 mandatory date before the phased timetable settled on October 2026 for receiving sites ([gov.uk DWT service page](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/digital-waste-tracking-service)). Treat the dates above as current at 8 August 2026 and re-check gov.uk before relying on them.

**I'm a carrier and a site operator — which date applies?**
Both. Your permitted site's receiving duty starts 1 October 2026; your carrier-side recording duty follows in October 2027.
