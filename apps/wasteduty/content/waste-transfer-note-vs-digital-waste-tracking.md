# Waste transfer notes vs digital waste tracking: what changes in October 2026

_Rules version: The Digital Waste Tracking (England) Regulations 2026 (draft SI as published on legislation.gov.uk) · Last reviewed: 8 August 2026_

If your site runs on paper transfer notes today, the October 2026 digital waste tracking (DWT) mandate is the biggest change to your paperwork since the duty of care arrived. Here's what actually changes — and what doesn't.

## The transfer note regime today

Under the duty of care in **EPA 1990 s.34** ([legislation.gov.uk](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/43/section/34)) and the **Waste (England and Wales) Regulations 2011** ([SI 2011/988](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2011/988)), a written description of the waste accompanies each transfer — the waste transfer note (or consignment note for hazardous waste). Both parties keep their copy; non-hazardous transfer notes are retained for two years `[VERIFY: retention period citation — reg. 35 of SI 2011/988]`.

## What DWT changes

From **1 October 2026**, operators of permitted facilities in England & Wales must record each load of controlled waste received in the DWT service **by the end of the second working day following receipt** ([The Digital Waste Tracking (England) Regulations 2026](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukdsi/2026/9780348282726)).

|                      | Paper transfer note                      | DWT receipt record                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Who creates it       | Transferor and transferee sign           | The **receiving site operator** records                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| When                 | At transfer                              | Within two working days of receipt                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Where it lives       | Filing cabinet / PDF folder              | Central government service                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Data                 | Description, EWC code, parties, SIC code | Fixed mandatory field set incl. physical form, container details, weight + estimated flag, D/R code, carrier registration number ([DEFRA receipt data definitions](https://defra.github.io/waste-tracking-service/production/)) |
| Visible to regulator | On request/inspection                    | On submission                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |

## What does not change

- **The duty of care itself.** EPA 1990 s.34 still requires you to take reasonable measures: waste transferred only to authorised persons, adequately described, not escaping your control.
- **Carrier checks.** Accepting waste only from registered carriers remains fundamental — the carrier registration number is a mandatory DWT field.
- **Permit conditions and returns.** Your environmental permit obligations run in parallel.

`[VERIFY: whether the 2026 Regulations disapply the SI 2011/988 written-description/transfer-note requirement for movements recorded in DWT, or whether both formally apply during transition — check the final SI's consequential amendments before publishing a "you can bin the paper" claim.]`

## Practical transition plan for a receiving site

1. Keep issuing/retaining transfer documentation as today until the position in the flag above is confirmed.
2. Rebuild your intake form around the DWT mandatory field list — the paper note doesn't capture everything DWT needs.
3. Start recording digitally in September as a dry run.
4. Keep the physical evidence (weighbridge tickets, photos) attached to each digital record — the DWT submission proves _that_ you recorded; your evidence proves _what actually happened_ if a load is disputed.
